# Exhibit design

The visual language uses a black base, clear globe, purple center glow, and four
animated violet spokes. A nearby screen can show only synthetic normalized
values: display pressure, glow level, frame rate, and elapsed time.

## Modes

- **Pumpdown:** blue ring slowly shifts toward violet as the synthetic pressure falls.
- **Star mode:** four spokes pulse around the center without representing real output.
- **Idle:** dim blue breathing light.
- **Stopped:** every pixel off.

## Accuracy statement

The model explains the general idea that charged particles are associated with
an electrostatic-fusor concept, but the light pattern and telemetry are artistic.
It should never display fabricated neutron counts, fusion rates, or experimental
claims. A college portfolio should call it a safe interactive exhibit or visual
model, not a functioning fusor.
