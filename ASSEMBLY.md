# Assembly

1. Install Raspberry Pi OS and confirm the simulation runs without hardware.
2. Mount the Pi and level shifter on insulated standoffs inside a ventilated base.
3. Mount the LED ring horizontally beneath the clear acrylic globe.
4. Add four translucent rods crossing at the center to diffuse the animated
   spokes. Keep them mechanically separate from the electronics.
5. Wire the low-voltage system exactly as shown in [WIRING.md](WIRING.md).
6. Test with a five-pixel software configuration before enabling the full ring.
7. Confirm the animation exits to all LEDs off.
8. Secure the globe without sealing, evacuating, or pressurizing it.
9. Add a label: “LED simulation—no plasma, fusion, or radiation.”

The globe is a decorative light diffuser, not a pressure or vacuum vessel.
